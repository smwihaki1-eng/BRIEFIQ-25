Fair questions

What's the time commitment?

As much or as little as your real work allows — most testers run a brief or two a week and send a few lines of feedback. There's no quota and no busywork.

Does it cost anything?

Your first five briefs are free, with every engine unlocked — no card to start. Send us feedback and we gift you three more — eight in total. After that you can keep going with Pro ($20/mo) or your own API key. BriefIQ never charges you by surprise; if it ever does, that's a bug — email the founder.

Where does my data go?

Your briefs and analyses stay in your own browser — BriefIQ has no server that sees them. The only thing we collect is what you choose to send in the feedback form.

How many people are in the cohort?

Twenty, deliberately. Small enough that your feedback actually moves things. When the spots are gone, you can still join the waitlist for the next round.

What happens when the beta ends?

You keep everything you made. Founding testers get grandfathered perks on whatever plan launches — details shared with the cohort first.
