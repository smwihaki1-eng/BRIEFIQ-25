AI Disclosure

What BriefIQ's analysis is, how it's made, and where its limits are. In plain language. ← back to BriefIQ
Last updated: 21 August 2026
A sharp second opinion — not the final word

BriefIQ is a decision-support tool for creative strategy. It's built to challenge a brief, surface tensions and show relevant work — the way a good Creative Director would. It is not a decision-maker, and it does not replace your professional judgment or a real expert. Read every output as a starting point to pressure-test, not an answer to copy.

1. What the analysis is — and isn't

BriefIQ produces a structured strategic read of a brief: the likely real problem, red flags, insights, opportunity gaps, creative territories and curated reference examples. It is a thinking aid. It is not a guarantee of results, a fact-checked report, or professional advice of any kind.

2. How the analysis is produced

  • The default engine is not a chatbot. BriefIQ's built-in engine is a deterministic, rules-and-frameworks system that runs entirely in your browser. It applies known strategy frameworks to your input — it does not "know" things beyond what you give it, and it does not browse the web.
  • Generative AI is optional and opt-in. If you connect your own AI model (your Claude, OpenAI or Google key) or use a managed AI plan, parts of the analysis are generated by a large language model. LLMs can be confidently wrong, can invent details ("hallucinate"), and reflect patterns in their training data — not verified truth. Treat anything AI-generated as a draft to verify.

3. Where it can be wrong

  • It only sees what you give it — a thin brief produces a thin, more assumption-heavy read.
  • It infers audience, market and objective when you don't state them; those inferences can miss.
  • Scores, percentages and confidence labels are directional heuristics, not measured outcomes.
  • Market, cultural and regulatory context changes faster than any curated library.

4. References & examples

The campaigns and case studies BriefIQ shows are a hand-picked, curated library, not live or exhaustive search results, and copyright-safe placeholders are used where no permitted image exists. Every reference links to "Find the original" so you can verify the real work and its source — please do, especially before citing it. Live, current web references require your own AI key.

5. When to consult a professional

BriefIQ is not a lawyer, accountant, doctor or regulator. Nothing here is legal, financial, medical, tax, regulatory or investment advice. For anything with legal, financial, safety, health or compliance consequences — contracts, claims you'll make publicly, regulated categories (finance, health, alcohol, children), data protection — get advice from a qualified professional before you act.

6. Your judgment stays in charge

You are responsible for what you do with BriefIQ's output. It's designed to make you think harder and faster — not to think for you. The best use is to argue with it: keep what holds up, discard what doesn't, and bring your own expertise, taste and knowledge of the client to every decision.
