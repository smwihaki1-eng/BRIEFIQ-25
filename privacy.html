Privacy Policy

Plain-language version of how BriefIQ handles your information. No dark patterns, no data brokering. ← back to BriefIQ
Last updated: 12 July 2026
Your brief stays on your device by default

BriefIQ's built-in strategy engine runs entirely in your browser. When you use it, your brief is never uploaded anywhere — there is no BriefIQ server that sees it. Your brief only leaves your device if you deliberately choose to send it to an AI model (see §2).

1. What we store, and where

Almost everything BriefIQ produces lives in your browser's local storage, on your device — not on our servers:

  • Your briefs, analyses and saved projects
  • Your settings, market preferences and learning profile
  • Any AI provider API keys you add (see §3)

Clearing your browser data erases all of it. We can't recover it for you, because we never had a copy. Export anything you want to keep.

2. When your brief leaves your device

Your brief text is transmitted off your device only in these cases, and only because you chose them:

  • You connect your own AI key (BYOK): your brief goes directly from your browser to that provider (OpenAI, Anthropic or Google) under your account and their privacy policy. It does not pass through BriefIQ.
  • You use "BriefIQ AI" (managed, paid plans): your brief is sent to our serverless proxy, which forwards it to an AI provider using our keys, returns the result, and does not retain the brief beyond the request.
  • Manual Mode: you copy the prompt yourself into whatever AI tool you already use. Nothing is sent by BriefIQ at all.

Choose the offline engine and your brief never travels. That is the default, and it is a real one.

3. API keys

If you add your own OpenAI, Anthropic or Google key, it is stored only in your browser's local storage and used only to call that provider directly. It is never sent to a BriefIQ server, never logged, and never shared. You can add, replace or remove a key at any time in AI Settings — removing it deletes it from your browser immediately.

4. Accounts & billing

Accounts and paid plans are handled by our processors when you use them: sign-in via Supabase (email, name), payments via Stripe (Stripe holds your card details — BriefIQ never sees or stores card numbers). We keep only your email, display name and current plan. If you never create an account, we hold none of this.

5. Waitlist

If you join a plan waitlist, we store the email you gave us and which plan you asked about, solely to notify you when it launches. Ask us to delete it any time.

6. What we don't do

  • No advertising trackers, no third-party analytics pixels, no selling or renting your data
  • No reading or training on your briefs
  • No hidden background uploads — the offline engine is genuinely offline

7. Your rights

You can export, delete or take your data with you at any time — most of it is already yours, sitting in your browser. For anything we hold (account, waitlist), email us and we'll delete it.
