05 — Motion

Keyframes: marq (marquee), rise (entrance), spinRing (asterisk), coilDraw (scribble draw-on), bob, wiggle, floaty, pulse.

Hover: small translates (−2 / −3px), hard-shadow reveals, colour swaps. Transitions .15–.22s.

All motion respects prefers-reduced-motion: reduce — global kill-switch + the mouse trail opts out on touch / reduced-motion.

06 — Voice

Diagnostic, confident, a little confrontational. "The brief is lying to you. We find the truth."

  • Diagnose-before-prescribe; "senior planning department" energy
  • Short declaratives, em-dashes; sentence-case body, UPPERCASE display
  • Honest about limits ("works offline, no keys needed")
  • Africa-first — "built from Nairobi to Jakarta, not adapted from New York"
07 — Do / Don't

✓ Do

  • Anton for display, never body; Space Mono only for micro-labels
  • One orange CTA per view; purple reserved for "Analyse a Brief"
  • Hard shadows, flat brights, 1.5px ink borders

✗ Don't

  • No gradients, no blurred shadows, no faux-bold Anton
  • Never claim "Powered by Stripe" until billing is live
  • No #3DFF7E or #8a8a83 text on light backgrounds
