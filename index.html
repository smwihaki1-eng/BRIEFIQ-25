✳ 01 — What BriefIQ does

How it
works

BriefIQ doesn't rush to generate ads. It works the way a senior team does — it studies your whole project first, challenges the brief, researches, and only then helps you think. Six steps, in order.

01
Build your project

Upload anything

Briefs, PDFs, decks, research, logos, packaging, screenshots, moodboards. BriefIQ reads the words and the colours out of every file — one connected project, not scattered attachments.

02
Project understanding

Confirm before it thinks

Before a single recommendation, BriefIQ shows what it understood — industry, audience, brand colours, gaps — and waits for you to confirm or correct it. Nothing is generated until it's right.

03
Challenge the brief

Interrogate, don't obey

What's missing, what's risky, which assumptions are hiding — what a Creative Director would question and a client would push back on. The brief gets pressure-tested before it's answered.

04
Research before thinking

Evidence, not opinions

Relevant, current work — campaigns, identities, UX, packaging — each with why it's recommended, what to learn, a confidence score and how it applies to your brief. Live when your own AI key is connected.

05
Generate intelligence

Tensions before ideas

Only now does it think — opportunities and tensions first, then distinct strategic territories, positioning, insights, production, media, UX and identity. Every output traced back to your project.

06
Present with confidence

Ready for the room

The output is presentation-ready — structured, evidence-backed and specific enough to take straight to a client, Creative Director or Executive Producer without a rewrite.
