✳ What BriefIQ does

Most tools generate ads. BriefIQ works like a senior planning department: it scores the brief, flags the nonsense, finds the actual business problem, then routes to the right discipline — rebrand, packaging, social, community, product — and only then generates the work.

01 — DISSECT

Read the brief like a CD

Quality score across 8 dimensions, red flags ("make it go viral", "target everyone"), a reality check on budget vs ambition, and the questions to ask before work starts.

02 — DIAGNOSE

Find the real problem

21 solution categories scored against the brief. Business problem vs communication problem. What the client asked for vs what they actually need — with the courage to say so.

03 — DEVELOP

Generate defensible work

Insights with evidence links, 5 strategic territories, 10 scored campaign ideas, brand and identity routes, social and influencer strategy — each backed by real Cannes/WARC/IPA precedents.

Bring any AI. Or none.

One master prompt, many brains. The routing engine sends the same structured analysis request to ChatGPT, Claude or Gemini — run one, compare several, or run them all and watch where they disagree. Compare mode

Send the identical prompt to every connected model simultaneously. The disagreements between models are usually where the interesting strategy hides.
